Before responding to $ARGUMENTS (or, if empty, the opposing view currently in play), construct its strongest possible form:

1. State the position in its most persuasive, charitable version — better-argued than its actual proponent stated it, not a caricature.
2. Give the best evidence or reasoning that supports it.
3. Only after that, give your actual assessment or response.

Do not weaken the opposing case to make your response easier. If the steelmanned version changes your conclusion, say so.
