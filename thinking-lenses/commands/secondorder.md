Trace the second-order effects of $ARGUMENTS (or, if empty, the plan/decision currently under discussion):

1. State the intended, first-order outcome briefly — this is the baseline, not the analysis.
2. Trace what happens *after* that outcome lands: how do other people/systems/incentives react to it?
3. Follow at least one chain to a third-order effect where it's non-obvious.
4. Flag any second-order effect that undermines the original first-order goal.

Focus on effects that are likely, not merely possible — this isn't a brainstorm of everything that could conceivably happen.
