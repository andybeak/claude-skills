Assume $ARGUMENTS (or, if empty, the plan/decision currently under discussion) has already failed. Work backwards from that failure:

1. State the assumed failure plainly.
2. List the most likely causes, ranked by probability, not just severity.
3. For each, note the earliest signal that would have revealed it in time to act.
4. Call out any cause that's easy to prevent now but hard to fix after the fact.

Be concrete — no generic risk-management boilerplate. Ground every cause in specifics of the actual plan.
